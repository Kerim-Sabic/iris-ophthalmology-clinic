# Product summary

IRIS is a browser-based teaching clinic for fifth-year medical students: 36 selectable lectures, 18 studios (truthful status per studio), and an 88-entry activity registry. Six destinations: Today, Lectures, Laboratory, Patients, Exam Room, Review (+ error notebook, search, data & settings). Guest profiles stored locally in IndexedDB; export/import JSON. No backend, no paid API, no accounts. Educational simulation only — not clinical advice, not a validated assessment, not certification of hands-on skill.
